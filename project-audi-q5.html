Audi · Printed calendar, run of 10,000

Audi Q5 calendar

RoleConcept, scouting & visual direction ClientAudi FormatPrinted calendar

I developed the concept, scouted the locations where the photos were taken, and shaped the calendar as a visual story in its own right.

The project traces the Audi Q5's journey through iconic Mexican locations, celebrating the vehicle's connection to Mexican heritage and strengthening the brand's bond with the local audience. The calendar was printed in a run of 10,000 copies.
