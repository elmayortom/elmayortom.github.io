A week in Antalya with 100 fundraisers

RolePhoto, video & social coverage ClientTemmel Fundraising LocationAntalya, Türkiye Attendees~100 participants

Once a year, Temmel Fundraising takes its team on a Seminarreise — a week-long trip built around training and team building for their door-to-door fundraisers. In 2025, that trip landed in Antalya, Türkiye, with around 100 fundraisers in attendance.

I was invited to document the entire week: photos, video, Reels, and Instagram Stories, capturing everything from sports activities and team-building exercises to skill-building seminars and the Abend Program — the evening parties, karaoke nights, and everything in between.

A week, in photos

These photos serve more than one purpose. They document the trip itself, help promote future events, and give Temmel a way to show what it's like to work there — which matters when you're trying to attract 18 to 25-year-olds considering a summer as a fundraiser. And for the fundraisers themselves, the photos become something to look forward to: a way to take a piece of the experience home with them.
