Alejandro Zamora

¡Hola! My name is Alejandro, a Mexican storyteller and content creator based in Germany.

Video, text, image — I like telling stories in every format, shape, and flavor.

It all started more than 10 years ago, while I was studying journalism. I published my first feature article in 2013, and picked up a camera for the first time at a music festival in 2015. The photos weren't great, but I've gotten better. I swear!

Ever since, I've worked in the automotive industry, the gaming sector, and creative agencies.

In 2018, I was awarded Mexico's National Sci-Fi & Fantasy Short Story Prize for a piece centered on linguistics. By the way, I love languages! I'm fluent in English, Spanish, German, and French.

Cover art by Yazz Casillas

Karl Bushby is a professional adventurer currently attempting the longest unbroken walk around the Earth in history. He started his quest in 1998, and hasn't been back home in England in almost 20 years.

I met him in 2020 in Guadalajara, in the middle of the pandemic. It's needless to say his journey is nothing short of extraordinary. So I decided to interview him and write a profile on his life, his journey, and the goals he still has left to reach. I pitched it to Magis Magazine in Mexico, and they ended up making it the cover story of their July issue.

Social Media Campaign · Concept + Employer Branding

Knock, Knock! Doormats spotted while door-to-door fundraising

Door-to-door fundraisers spend their days walking from house to house, and along the way, they run into all kinds of doormats — funny, weird, occasionally judgmental. I started collecting them.

What began as a one-off joke turned into a recurring social media series, closing out each Instagram carousel with a photo the team sent in from the field. It's since become part of the brand identity of Temmel Fundraising, a door-to-door fundraising agency that hires young people in German-speaking countries to sign up new donors for social projects.

It's a small, unexpected way to show what that work actually looks like, one welcome mat at a time.
