Alejandro Zamora

¡Hola! My name is Alejandro, a Mexican author and storyteller based in Germany.

Video, text, image — I like telling stories in every format, shape, and flavor.

It all started more than 10 years ago, while I was studying journalism. I published my first feature article in 2013, and picked up a camera for the first time at a music festival in 2015. The photos weren't great, but I've gotten better. I swear.

Ever since, I've worked as a content creator in the automotive industry, the gaming sector, and creative agencies.

In 2018, I was awarded Mexico's National Sci-Fi & Fantasy Short Story Prize. I'm fluent in English, Spanish, German, and French.
