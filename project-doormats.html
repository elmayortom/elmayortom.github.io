Knock, Knock! Doormats spotted while door-to-door fundraising

RoleConcept & Graphic design FormatInstagram series ClientTemmel Fundraising

Door-to-door fundraisers spend their days walking from house to house, and along the way, they run into all kinds of doormats — funny, weird, occasionally judgmental. I started collecting the photos as a way to show the personality of the neighborhoods the team visited.

What began as a one-off joke turned into a recurring social media series, closing out each Instagram carousel with a photo the team sent in from the field. It's since become part of the brand's informal voice and helped humanize the work.

It's a small, unexpected way to show what that work actually looks like, one welcome mat at a time.

check out @temmelfundraising on Instagram to see the full collection.
