Knock, Knock! Doormats spotted while door-to-door fundraising

RoleConcept & content direction FormatInstagram series + video ReachGerman-speaking countries

I noticed that door-to-door fundraisers often come across curious doormats during their daily rounds, and turned it into a recurring social media series.

Each Instagram carousel closes out with a photo the team sent in. It's since become part of our brand identity.
