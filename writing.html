Stories about language, some true, some not

LanguagesSpanish · English · German RecognitionNational SciFi & Fantasy Award, MX 2018

I love languages. It's not a coincidence that the very first story I ever published was about Esperanto, the artificial language invented by Zamenhof in the 19th century. Ever since that feature appeared in a local newspaper in 2013, I've been building a collection of stories around this same topic — something the linguist Michel Erard once called language journalism.

In 2018, I was awarded Mexico's National Sci-Fi & Fantasy Short Story Prize for a story about — you guessed it — linguistics.

This page is a compilation of that work. Some pieces are non-fiction. Some are fiction. All of it, a passion project through and through.
